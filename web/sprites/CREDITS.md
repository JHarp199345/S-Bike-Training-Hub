# Course animals

Sprite strips rendered in Blender (from `art_src/`, kept out of the repo) from 3D models by
**Quaternius**, all released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain): https://quaternius.com

- Farm Animals pack: horse, zebra, cow, pig, sheep, llama, pug (the unicorn is the horse with a horn added)
- Animal Pack Vol.2: dog, cat, wolf, eagle
- Animals Pack: chick, red fox, bird
- Animated Monster Pack: dragon, bat

CC0 needs no credit; this is a thank-you. If you like them: https://www.patreon.com/quaternius
