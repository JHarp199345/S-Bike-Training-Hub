S-Bike Training Hub

Train every sport without breaking down.

A free, local training hub for triathletes and multisport athletes. It measures the load each part of your body is carrying (heart and lungs, bones and tendons, the muscles you use) and matches it to how fast each one recovers, so your progress stays steady instead of two steps forward and one back.

HOW IT WORKS

1

You train and report

Your fitness tracker records every session. A quick morning check-in, a follow-up after lifting and a Sunday look back say how your body actually feels.

2

The hub measures the load

Heart-and-lungs load for every sport, and mechanical load tissue by tissue: running blocks for bones and tendons (slow to heal), leg muscles, shoulders from swimming, each muscle you lift with.

3

It learns your recovery

Every model starts from a careful guess and tunes itself to your reports, so the numbers become yours: how much you can take, and how long each part of you needs.

4

Your AI plans with it

Your AI assistant reads the numbers and plans your days: when to push, when to ease off, and what kind of session fits. You decide; your call always wins.

BUILT TO WORK WITH AN AI ASSISTANT

The hub keeps the numbers; your AI does the coaching conversation. It works with a frontier model (like Claude) or a local model running free on your own computer, through any app that supports MCP servers. Everything stays on your Mac.

🧭 Consult me

"Look at my training load and plan me a lifting session that fits my triathlon plan for the next three weeks. Let's discuss it."

Your AI leads with your numbers and the hub's suggestions, and you shape the plan together.

📋 I have a plan

"I have a workout: goblet squats 3 × 8 at 35, dead hangs 3 × 30 seconds…"

Your AI records it as you said it and scores it, so the load counts. Your plan stands.

Connect it (after setup):

claude mcp add --scope user s-bike-hub -- "…/.venv/bin/python" "…/mcp_server.py"

Claude Desktop and other MCP apps: install io.github.JHarp199345/s-bike-hub from the MCP Registry, or the one-file bundle from the GitHub releases. More in Training with your AI.

WHAT YOU NEED

⌚ A fitness trackerAny watch that exports activity files (.fit or .tcx): COROS, Garmin and most others. Your training load is built from what it records.
🤖 An AI assistantFrontier or local. A local model costs nothing to run.
🚴 A smart bikeOptional. Without one, the hub is your multisport coach; with one (tested on a Merach S29), it runs your rides too: hills, gears, ERG workouts and 3D routes.
