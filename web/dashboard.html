What these numbers mean

Load (TSS) - how much a ride took out of you: time × intensity², measured against your FTP (). An hour flat out at FTP is 100; an hour easy in zone 2 is about 40-50.

NP (normalized power) - your watts weighted for the surges, since hard bits cost more than their average. IF is NP ÷ FTP: 0.65-0.75 is endurance, around 1.0 is a race.

Fitness is your load averaged over about 6 weeks: it rises slowly with steady riding. Fatigue is the last week's: it jumps after hard days and drops fast with rest. Form is the difference. Around −10 to −30 means you're training; fresh (above +5) is when to race or test.

Only rides on the bike bridge count here: runs, swims and gym sessions aren't included, and fitness builds from your first bridge ride, so it starts small.
