# Haunted Graveyard scenery

Graveyard Kit 5.0 models and character animations by Kenney: https://kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit
Licensed CC0. Original license is included as LICENSE.txt.

Three.js 0.186.1 (MIT): https://threejs.org/
Source license is included at ../vendor/three.LICENSE.txt.

The Hub supplies the winding course, scenery placement, rider, lighting,
and encounter choreography. These are visual effects; they do not change a
workout, control a trainer, infer athletic capacity, or create activity records.
All dependencies and models are served locally; no third-party account is needed.
