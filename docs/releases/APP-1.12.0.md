# App 1.12.0 — Plan by hand, your reports decide, lifting classifiers

2026-10-12. Use with MCP 1.12.0.

Update: if you installed with `uvx s-bike-training-hub@latest`, restart your assistant app. Claude Desktop bundle users: install `s-bike-hub-mcp-1.12.0.mcpb` from the release.

## Plan by hand on a day grid

Each phase is a Monday-to-Sunday grid of its days: click or shift-click days and pick a session type; copy, paste and fill forward with a preview before anything is replaced; undo and redo. Saved workouts show on it.

## One goal field

Type a goal, or paste an event's web address and the hub fills in the name, the peak date, the planning horizon and the sport.

## Your program on the calendar

Planned sessions show faded on every day until the week's workouts are written.

## Your reports decide

The daily verdicts come from what you report (feet, legs, shoulders, breathing, illness, the overnight watch reading). Load ratios, recovery blocks and lifting carry-over are shown as evidence to weigh. Two sessions on the same tissue in a day are a caution judged by the combined load.

## Lifting by type, duration, intensity and style

Sessions are described by how many muscle groups they work, exercises per group, intensity (light 30-60%, medium 61-80%, heavy 81-105% of each lift's estimated max; never maxing) and style, each lift judged against what you moved last time. The assistant designs a session in one call and the hub suggests the intensity from your phase and your own responses.

## A faster assistant connection

Planning reads come as a short brief with sections on request; programs and reviews are compact by default; the server's instructions are a short core with topic guides. The same planning conversation uses about a fifth of the data.

## Hub-logged lifts take session reports

No watch file needed.
